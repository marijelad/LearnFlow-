How to build a learning system you can maintain

The best study plan is not the most ambitious one. It is the one that still works when the week becomes busy.

Start with a minimum viable session

Choose a session small enough that you can start without negotiating with yourself. Twenty to thirty minutes is often enough to review one concept, practice it, and leave a clear next step.

Consistency becomes easier when the next action is obvious and small.

Make practice visible

Instead of only tracking time, track outputs: one solved problem, one query, one component, one paragraph, or one explanation in your own words.

Use a simple loop

  1. Recall what you learned last time.
  2. Learn one new idea.
  3. Use it in a small task.
  4. Check the result.
  5. Write the next step before stopping.

Review the system, not just yourself

If you repeatedly skip sessions, change the environment, schedule, or session size. A learning plan is a design problem as much as a motivation problem.
