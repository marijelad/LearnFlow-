About LearnFlow

Learning should become usable.

LearnFlow is a fictional education platform designed as a front-end portfolio project. Its product idea is simple: reduce friction between watching a lesson and applying a skill.

Our product principles

Clarity over volume. Practice over passive consumption. Progress over streak-chasing.

Useful by design

Every course points toward a concrete outcome, project, decision, or repeatable skill.

Small enough to continue

Lessons are organized so a learner can make visible progress in focused sessions.

Feedback matters

Quizzes, projects, and progress signals help learners notice gaps while they are still easy to fix.
