Functions as reusable thinking

Functions help you name a piece of logic once and reuse it with different inputs.

def course_progress(completed, total): return round(completed / total * 100) print(course_progress(17, 25)) # 68

Think in inputs and outputs

A function is easier to understand when you focus on the contract: what goes in, what transformation happens, and what comes back out.

Try it

Change the values, predict the result before running the code, then explain why your prediction was right or wrong.
